About us

Guided by 32 years in the field

Om Agrotech is an Agri Clinic and Agri Business Centre (ACABC) founded by Mr. Hemant Kapase, a horticulture graduate with 32 years of industry experience.

Before starting Om Agrotech, the founder worked with Bajaj Tempo and Essar Agrotech, and has served as visiting faculty for MITCON and MCED training programmes and at the RBI College of Agriculture Banking.

As a certified Chartered Valuer for agriculture empanelled with local banking institutions, we understand a project from both sides of the table — the farmer’s and the banker’s.

  • Practical advice, not just paperwork
  • Plans that banks and government schemes accept
  • Support from idea to completion

Our services

Everything your horticulture project needs

Whether you are starting a nursery, building a polyhouse or planting an orchard, we take care of planning, paperwork and funding.

Pre-feasibility survey

A detailed survey and a complete horticulture development plan, so you know what will work on your land before you invest.

  • Land and resource survey
  • Crop and technology options
  • Cost and return estimates

Site evaluation

Soil and water analysis, weather assessment and infrastructure review to choose the right crop and the right design.

  • Soil and water testing
  • Weather and climate study
  • Road, power and infrastructure review

Polyhouse & shade-net

Planning and setting up greenhouses, polyhouses and shade-net houses for vegetables, flowers and nursery plants.

  • Layout and structure design
  • Crop selection for protected farming
  • Guidance on materials and set-up

Fruit plantations

High-density and ultra-high-density orchards that give more yield from the same acre.

  • Variety and rootstock selection
  • Spacing and planting plan
  • Irrigation and orchard management plan

Nursery establishment

Setting up horticulture nurseries for fruit, vegetable and ornamental plants.

  • Layout and capacity planning
  • Propagation and mother-plant guidance
  • Fruit, vegetable and ornamental plants

Nursery accreditation

Guidance for nursery accreditation and certification, so your plants earn buyers’ trust.

  • Eligibility check
  • Documentation support
  • Preparation for inspection

Bankable project reports

Clear, well-researched reports prepared the way banks and funding agencies expect to see them.

  • Project cost and means of finance
  • Market and viability analysis
  • Formats that banks ask for

Government funding

Help with subsidy and loan applications under NHB, NHM, APEDA and NABARD schemes.

  • Scheme eligibility check
  • Application and supporting documents
  • Follow-up with the agency

Agriculture valuation

Valuation of farms, orchards and agricultural assets by a Govt. approved chartered valuer.

  • Farms, orchards and nurseries
  • Valuation for bank and loan use
  • Report by a Govt. approved chartered valuer

Online consultation

Not in Pune? Talk to us by phone or video call, wherever your farm is.

  • Phone and video consultation
  • Remote review of your documents
  • Marathi, Hindi and English
